Aces & Eights Collector's Edition Poker Set

The Franklin Mint Aces & Eights Collector's Edition poker set, an official issue of the Western Heritage Museum.

Two decks of playing cards and 200 gaming chips with the look of gold and silver, in a custom hardwood and brass display cabinet. Four separate racks hold the chips, and a drawer holds the cards. Comes with The Franklin Mint certification.
